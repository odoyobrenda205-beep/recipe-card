How to prepare pancakes


  • flour
  • eggs
  • milk
  • baking powder
  • sugar
  • cooking oil
  • water
    •
  1. break the eggs then whisk them
  2. add milk to eggs and continue whisking
  3. add sugar and a qountified amount of oil while whisking
  4. add flour in intervals while still whisking until you get a uniform homogenous mixture
  5. you can add a bit of water if necessary ,if the mixture is too heavy
  6. fry your mixture into pancakes
